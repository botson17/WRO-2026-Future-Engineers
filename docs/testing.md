## Planned tests

1. Mechanical inspection: dimensions, mass, freely turning wheels and repeatable steering angles.
2. Sensor calibration under the actual lighting and surface conditions used for each trial.
3. Straight driving and turns in both directions at several speeds.
4. Three autonomous laps with changed inner walls for the Open Challenge.
5. Red and green traffic sign handling at multiple legal positions for the Obstacle Challenge.
6. Three laps plus parallel parking with parking-space variations.
7. Repeat trials after each chassis, gearing, mounting or software change.

Record the number of runs, exact field configuration, success criteria, failures and measured timing. Upload photos or videos as evidence and link the corresponding code commit. Do not enter expected performance as if it were a measured result.
