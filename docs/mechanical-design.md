# Mobility and mechanical design

## Current proposal

A custom 3D-printed flat chassis plate carries a LEGO-based drive and a LEGO gear-driven steering mechanism for the front wheels. This is a design proposal pending assembly and measurements.

## Complete after building

- Overall length, width, height, weight, wheelbase, track width, ground clearance and center of mass estimate.
- Labeled chassis drawings, CAD files, print material, infill and fastener details.
- Photos of the steering mechanism and linkage at straight, full left and full right positions.
- Drive axle connection, gear ratios, motor and wheel models, wheel diameter and tire choice.
- Steering actuator, ratio, achieved wheel angles, backlash and turning radius.
- Torque and speed estimates, assumptions, measured straight-line speed and hill/stall behavior if tested.
- Assembly instructions and a bill of materials with exact quantities.
- Alternatives tried, mechanical failures and the reason for each iteration.

## Design checks

Check dimensions and mass against section 11 of the [2026 rules](https://wro-association.org/wp-content/uploads/WRO-2026-Future-Engineers-Self-Driving-Cars-General-Rules.pdf). Confirm four wheels, a mechanically connected drive axle and one steering actuator. Test whether the gear steering can turn without the wheels hitting the printed chassis. Check that the final car fits the variable parking space.
